# Shot 001 — Arrival Boulevard

## Purpose

First visual proof that BLACK SIGNAL works as a film.

The shot must establish three things immediately:

1. District 12 is large.
2. District 12 is empty.
3. District 12 is still alive.

## Frame

Static establishing frame first. Only add camera motion after the still works.

- lens/FOV target: 55°
- camera height: human eye level, approximately 1.7 m
- roll: 0°
- road: lower third, strong vanishing point
- skyline: upper third, partially swallowed by haze
- dominant horizon feature: one overpass / elevated structure / tower
- left/right masses: intentionally asymmetric
- foreground: one crosswalk, lane marking, or directional arrow must read clearly
- population: zero visible people

## Lighting / atmosphere

The target is early morning, not night-cyberpunk.

- local time: 07:43
- cool blue-gray atmospheric fog
- low-saturation concrete / asphalt / metal
- restrained emissive signage
- controlled bloom
- no mandatory rain
- distant architecture should lose contrast before it loses silhouette

## Sound cue

City PA, neutral and routine:

> Good morning, District Twelve. Local time is 07:43. Current population: zero.

Do not score the line as a horror reveal. Let the wording do the work.

## Impossible detail

After the word `zero`, hold the empty frame for a beat.

One advert panel changes for less than a second:

> I KNOW YOU'RE THERE

It returns to its normal loop before the audience can comfortably process it.

No other supernatural event occurs in this shot.

## Cut condition

Cut only after the advert has returned to normal and the city once again appears inert.

## Acceptance test

Mute the shot and view a still frame. It should already communicate:

- large city
- morning
- abandonment
- technological infrastructure still operating

Then restore audio. The PA line and advert glitch should turn the image from abandoned-city imagery into the premise of BLACK SIGNAL.
