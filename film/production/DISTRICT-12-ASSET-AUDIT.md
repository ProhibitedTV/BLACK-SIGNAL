## Production layout

District 12 should be treated as a virtual backlot rather than one contiguous gameplay map.

### Zone A — Arrival Boulevard

Primary opening location.

- longest clean road sightline in the map
- tall background buildings on both sides
- one elevated overpass in the middle/far distance
- sparse lamps and active signage
- skyline forms fading into morning haze
- enough empty foreground to emphasize that the population is gone

### Zone B — Service Canyon

- tight side street / alley
- vents, cables, pipes, trash, service doors
- used for compression, tension, and dialogue coverage

### Zone C — Municipal Plaza

- wide civic space
- one dominant municipal/corporate structure
- ideal location for protagonist arrival or later reveal

### Zone D — Utility Underpass

- concrete and infrastructure-heavy environment
- barriers, power hardware, cables, service access
- useful for darker mid-film material without building a separate level

## Shot 001 target

The first production milestone is not "finish the city." It is one convincing establishing shot.

**Shot 001:** static or extremely slow push along Arrival Boulevard at 07:43.

Audio:

> Good morning, District Twelve. Local time is 07:43. Current population: zero.

Required frame elements:

- road in foreground
- readable crosswalk or lane marking
- modular buildings framing both sides
- one distant elevated structure
- active advert or emissive signage
- blue-gray morning atmosphere
- no visible people
- one subtle impossible detail after the announcement
