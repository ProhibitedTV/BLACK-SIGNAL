# BLACK SIGNAL
## Opening Sequence — CURRENT POPULATION: ZERO

**FADE IN:**

Black.

A low electrical hum. Far away, the metallic clunk of a transit system waking up.

A calm MUNICIPAL VOICE speaks in the dark.

**MUNICIPAL VOICE (V.O.)**  
Good morning, District Twelve. Local time is 07:43. Current population: zero.

A pause.

**MUNICIPAL VOICE (V.O.)**  
Air quality is acceptable. Surface transit is operating on reduced schedule. Have a productive day.

## EXT. DISTRICT TWELVE — CENTRAL AVENUE — MORNING

A vast cyberpunk boulevard disappears into blue-gray haze.

No people. No traffic.

A pedestrian signal changes from red to green.

Nobody crosses.

An autonomous street sweeper glides through frame, turns precisely at an empty curb, and continues out of sight.

A gigantic commercial display wakes above the street.

A smiling MODEL rotates into view beside a product slogan.

The image tears for three frames.

**I KNOW YOU'RE THERE**

The advertisement resumes.

A train passes high above the avenue. Most of its windows are dark.

## EXT. DISTRICT TWELVE — ENTRY CHECKPOINT — MORNING

A solitary figure approaches through the haze: **MARA VALE**, 30s, municipal systems technician, field coat over work clothes, compact diagnostic case in one hand.

She stops at an unmanned checkpoint.

A scanner wakes before she touches it.

**CHECKPOINT**  
Good morning, Mara Vale.

Mara looks at the dark guard booth.

**MARA**  
Morning.

She immediately regrets answering an empty booth.

The gate opens.

Six seconds after she passes through, the checkpoint speaks again.

**CHECKPOINT**  
Identity confirmed.

Mara turns back.

The gate is already closing.

## EXT. COMMERCIAL WALKWAY — CONTINUOUS

Mara walks deeper into the district.

Her boots echo between towers.

Storefront shutters are down, but internal lights click on one by one ahead of her.

Not behind her.

Ahead.

She stops.

The sequence of lights stops too.

Mara looks toward a convenience kiosk across the street.

Its coffee machine begins brewing.

A paper cup drops into place.

Steam curls upward.

Mara crosses to it.

The machine dispenses exactly one coffee.

A display above the machine illuminates.

**GOOD MORNING, MARA.**

Below it:

**YOUR USUAL?**

Mara stares.

She has never been here.

Her radio crackles.

**DISPATCH (V.O.)**  
Vale, status.

Mara keeps looking at the coffee.

**MARA**  
Inside the district.

**DISPATCH (V.O.)**  
Any visible activity?

Mara watches the steam.

**MARA**  
Depends what you mean by activity.

The kiosk screen glitches.

For less than a second, Mara sees security-camera footage of herself standing at the kiosk.

Same pose.

Same coat.

Timestamp:

**07:49:12**

Mara checks her wrist display.

**07:43:18**

She looks back at the kiosk.

The normal interface has returned.

A beat.

Somewhere inside an apartment tower, a telephone begins ringing.

Then another.

Then another.

One by one, windows across the block illuminate.

Mara looks up.

**CUT TO TITLE:**

# BLACK SIGNAL

**END OPENING SEQUENCE**
