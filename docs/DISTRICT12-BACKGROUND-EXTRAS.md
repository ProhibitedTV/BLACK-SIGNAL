# District 12 background extras

The production level includes 216 unarmed civilian extras across 36 blocks: 144 walkers and 72 stationary shoppers/bystanders. Six installed male/female character variants from the MAX collection supply clothing, faces, skeletons and materials. No character meshes or textures are vendored.

Each block has its own closed walking circuit and six named extras. A grid path search finds routes entirely inside that block's 1,200-unit paved parcel, avoiding measured street furniture and building envelopes with an 18-unit body radius plus planning clearance. Walkers do not cross roads, use free-roaming AI, or choose their own destinations. Their spawn positions are distributed around the circuit. Block direction, speed and pause duration vary. Two stationary extras provide shopfront and quieter-sidewalk activity.

`bs_city_extra.lua` controls movement along the validated line segments and uses the installed characters' `Walk_Loop` and `Idle` animations. Turns ease, long segments end with brief pauses, and a capped time step avoids jumps after a stalled frame. Missing walk animations freeze the actor in idle rather than sliding a static pose. Combat behavior and saved weapon overrides are removed. These are cinematic, nonblocking background extras: their collision is disabled so the player cannot push them into traffic, and they have no combat or interaction behavior.

The exact civilian records come from `horseshoe bend.fpm` (ELE 338), with only the supported default schema extension to ELE 342. Their material payloads remain intact. `tools/fpm_prepare_extras.py` can regenerate the template library from the installed demo and the immutable human-corner reference. The generated route table binds actors by unique `BS_EXTRA_###` names rather than mutable entity indices. Changing the layout requires rebuilding the FPM and its matching `.routes.lua` sidecar together. The deployment script validates that match and backs up/copies both scripts to project and global runtime folders with the main level.

Tests sample every route segment against pavement and obstacle bounds. A ten-minute simulation runs the actual Lua behavior for all 216 extras, checking that all walkers move, stationary extras stay put, every position remains on a validated route, stalled frames do not jump, and missing walk animations fail to idle. This is behavioral simulation, not proof of native rendering or animation quality. Native appearance, material loading, foot contact, gait speed and frame rate still require GameGuru MAX review. The level now contains 9,217 entities; 216 animated characters may require density tuning for particular shots and hardware.

The original empty-city story notes remain historical production context. This population pass follows the requested inhabited-city direction.
